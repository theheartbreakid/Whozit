## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a dark, luminous, and ethereal aesthetic built upon the foundation of Material 3 Expressive principles. It delivers an immersive, tactile experience designed for modern collaborative workflows, household management, and shared accountability applications.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
The visual language communicates effortless coordination, cosmic calm, and hyper-modern precision. By pairing deep twilight environments with volumetric ambient light fills, the UI evokes warmth, emotional balance, and clarity—transforming routine administrative scheduling into an ambient, tactile ritual.

### Design Movement
The style synthesizes **Glassmorphism**, **Material 3 Expressive**, and **Luminous Ambient UI**:
- **Layered Frosted Translucency:** Stacked chromatic planes with ultra-soft backdrop blurs simulate optical glass floating in dark space.
- **Configurable Ambient Radiance:** Soft, directional atmospheric glow fills (cyan, violet, and sunset rose) project through container backdrops, simulating physical light emission.
- **Organic Expressive Geometry:** Playful interplay of pill-shaped indicators, full-stadium buttons, and deeply rounded cards (`24px` to `36px`) establishing welcoming, friendly visual boundaries.

## Colors

The palette operates in a native dark mode rooted in deep interstellar blue-blacks, overlaid with saturated, luminous spectral accents.

### Tonal Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#090B13`):** Ultimate backdrop evoking twilight depths.
- **Surface Container Lowest (`#0D101C`):** Recessed slots, search fields, and inactive wells.
- **Surface Container (`#111422`):** Standard container surface with low-opacity alpha layers (`rgba(17, 20, 34, 0.72)`).
- **Surface Container High (`#181C2E`):** Raised interactive cards and actionable elements with subtle internal glow.
- **Surface Container Highest (`#22273F`):** Floating sheets, popovers, and elevated modal panels.

### Expressive Accents & Glow Roles
- **Primary Violet Spectrum (`#8B5CF6`, `#A78BFA`, `#6366F1`):** Represents focused ownership, active schedule turns, and primary triggers.
- **Secondary Cyan Teal (`#06B6D4`, `#38BDF8`):** Applied to connected statuses, ambient atmospheric pools, and supportive metadata.
- **Tertiary Sunset / Coral Highlight (`#F43F5E`, `#FB7185`):** Reserved for urgent interventions, destructive confirmations, and sunset edge-lighting accents.
- **On-Surface High Contrast (`#F1F5F9`):** Primary text and focal iconography ensuring crisp legibility over tinted glass.
- **On-Surface Medium (`#94A3B8`):** Secondary contextual cues, relative timestamps, and resting state icons.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, leaning into its balanced geometric proportions, wide aperture, and contemporary rounded character.

### Structural Discipline
- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) preserve cohesion against high-blur glass surfaces and radiant background gradients.
- **Hero Metrics:** Display styles prioritize high-contrast white text (`#F8FAFC`) with generous line-heights to eliminate optical crowding next to luminous avatars and badge components.
- **Body & Labels:** Slightly widened letter-spacing in labels ensures extreme clarity on small chip surfaces, floating dock tabs, and metadata badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a fluid, touch-first responsive grid system engineered for handheld and compact tablet navigation.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Rhythm Multiplier:** Standard spacing conforms to an active `4px` / `8px` baseline rhythm.
- **Screen Margins:** Fixed `16px` (`margin`) gutters along screen peripheries on compact mobile devices, scaling up to `24px` (`margin-md`) on larger viewports.
- **Component Padding:** Cards apply a signature interior padding of `space-lg` (`20px`) for spacious readability, with compact sub-tiles using `space-md` (`12px`).
- **Floating Dock Inset:** Persistent navigation docks maintain a `16px` bottom safety offset floating clean off the screen boundary, ensuring clear spatial stratification between actionable content and system gestures.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses optical physical layers instead of sharp drop shadows:

### Translucent Glass Stacking
Depth is achieved via multi-layered semi-transparent surfaces with progressive backdrop blurs:
- **Level 1 (Subsurface Cards):** `rgba(24, 28, 46, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an inner micro-border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Prominent Cards & Controls):** `rgba(34, 39, 63, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a directional light reflection along the top border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals, FABs, Floating Navigation):** `rgba(17, 20, 34, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, wrapped in a perimeter hairline gradient stroke.

### Chromatic Ambient Radiance
- Elements do not cast muddy black shadows; they project colored ambient light cones. Primary triggers emit an aura: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`.
- Cards incorporate configurable atmospheric radial gradients positioned behind glass layers (e.g. `radial-gradient(circle at 100% 0%, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25), transparent 70%)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a pill-centric, hyper-curved geometry in accordance with Material 3 Expressive standards:
- **Interactive Controls:** Buttons, search containers, segmented filters, and navigation islands use full-pill styling (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Standard Structural Tiles:** Content containers, list items, and settings groups use `24px` to `28px` corner radiuses (`rounded-xl`).
- **Hero & Dialog Surfaces:** Major hero turn cards, bottom sheets, and alert dialogues incorporate large `32px` to `36px` rounded corners.
- **Asymmetric Feature Accents:** Signature status badges and dynamic turn indicators pair soft rectangular corners with hemispherical pill segments to denote dynamic state transitions.

## Components

### Buttons & Split Triggers
- **Filled Primary:** Gradient fill transitioning from `#8B5CF6` to `#6366F1`, full pill radius, `16px` vertical height padding, subtle inset top shine `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`, emitting an active violet ambient glow.
- **Split Actions:** Unified pill container containing a leading text button coupled to a vertical translucent divider (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) and a trailing dropdown chevron trigger.
- **Tonal / Ghost:** High-translucency pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), white medium text, elevating on hover with subtle border luminance.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Stateful Selectors:** Segmented bar containers featuring recessed track backgrounds (`#0D101C`). The active item transitions into a frosted gradient pill with spring physics.
- **Avatar Badges:** Circular avatar clusters with high-contrast `2px` surface borders paired with luminous status dot pins (`#10B981` active, `#F43F5E` alert).

### Cards & Schedules
- **Active Turn Hero Card:** Features rich directional sunset-to-midnight mesh gradient backgrounds (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35), rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15) 50%, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1) 100%)`). Includes an ethereal concentric circle halo framing the primary turn icon.
- **List Item Tiles:** Enclosed within `24px` translucent capsules with internal paddings of `16px`, complete with trailing metadata pills and circular icon prefixes.

### Inputs & Sliders
- **Search Fields:** Low-profile capsules with inset background tone, leading translucent icon (`#94A3B8`), and subtle focus transitions featuring a cyan-to-purple outline glow.
- **Expressive Track Sliders:** Broad, ergonomic pill tracks with high-glow accent thumb indicators and gradient-filled progress runs.

### Floating Dock Navigation
- An elevated horizontal stadium bar floating anchored at the bottom viewport center.
- Houses icon-label pairs with dynamic active state pills: the active tab transitions to a glowing tinted badge with subtle ambient backlight.

### Modals & Bottom Sheets
- Presented with a top drag handle pill, edge-to-edge backdrop blur overlays (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), and actionable destructive buttons rendered in luminous coral (`#F43F5E`).
